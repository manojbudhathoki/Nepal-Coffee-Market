Our purpose

A home for Nepal-grown coffee.

We want the people behind Nepali coffee to be easier to discover, understand, and support.

Our mission

We are building a discovery platform that brings Nepali coffee producers, farms, cooperatives, processors, roasters, products, and brewing knowledge together in one place.

Instead of replacing a producer's website, we help people find it. Listings can link directly to official websites and provide a starting point for conversations about availability, pricing, wholesale supply, and export.

We believe a strong coffee future should recognise farmers, communicate origin honestly, and make it easier for domestic and international buyers to discover local businesses.

“Coffee is not fashion, it's our tradition.”

An independent discovery project by Manoj Budhathoki.
